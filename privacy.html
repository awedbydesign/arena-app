The Arena Privacy Policy

Awed by Design LLC. Last updated November 8, 2026.

What the app is

The Arena is a disc golf throwing game. You draw shot cards, throw, and enter scores. You can play on one phone or host a lobby so other players join from their own phones.

What we collect

  • Player names and scores. The names you type and the scores you enter are stored on your phone and, in lobby games, sent to our game server (Google Firebase) so other players in your lobby can see them.
  • Location. If you turn on the GPS features, the app reads your phone's location to measure throws and to look up local wind conditions. Location is only used while you play, and the app works without it. You can turn it off any time in your phone settings.
  • Game logs. Finished games are saved on your phone and to our game server so leagues and leaderboards can work.

What we do not collect

No account or password, no email address, no contacts, no photos, no payment information, no advertising identifiers. We do not sell or share your information with anyone for marketing.

Third party services

  • Google Firebase hosts the app and the live lobby data.
  • Open-Meteo provides wind data when GPS features are on. Only your approximate coordinates are sent.

Deleting your data

Clearing the app's data on your phone removes everything stored locally. To have lobby or league data removed from our server, email us and include the player name you used.

Children

The Arena is for everyone, but it is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
