How to Play
The Arena · Disc Golf Throwing Game
Try it first: virtual walkthrough on awedbydesign.com →

Individual Sport

1v1
Everyone competes against everyone
No teams. All players score individually. Highest total wins.
8
Cards drawn at game start, all 8, no redraw
4 shots for H1, 4 shots for H2. Both halves are set before the first throw. Sides switch at halftime, shots do not change.
2
Throws per player per round
Every active player throws twice, both from the Tee zone. The Mando is a gate to aim through on the way to the basket, not a throwing position.

Sides and Halftime

2-5
Everyone throws all 8 rounds, switching ends at halftime
Rounds 1-4 from the Side A end. At halftime pick up your discs, walk to the Side B end, and throw rounds 5-8 back the other way. Bring 8 discs: a thrown disc stays down range until the switch.
6+
Two sides: split across the arena
Side A throws H1 (rounds 1-4), Side B throws H2 (rounds 5-8). Players switch sides at halftime.
HT
Halftime: switch sides, no redraw
Sides swap physical positions. H2 shots were already set at game start. Side B throws their 4 shots, Side A spots. One overall leaderboard. Highest individual total wins.

Scoring Per Throw

5
Basket
Disc lands in the basket from any zone.
3
Bullseye
Disc hits or lands within 1 meter (9.84 ft) of the center bullseye target.
2
Circle 1
Disc lands within 10 meters (32.8 ft) of the basket.
1
Mando
Disc passes through the correct mando gap.
0
Miss
No zone reached. Better luck next throw.

The Card

Tee Zone → Mando Gap → Basket Zone
Each card defines one unique shot. 108 total cards across 3 decks.

Decks

G
Green: Short · 18 cards
Every shot starts from the Front tee. 200 to 354 ft total. Beginners and tight spaces.
Y
Yellow: Mid · 53 cards
Any shot 360 ft and under, from any tee. All the Front shots plus the shorter Mid and Back tee shots.
R
Red: Long · 108 cards
Every card in the game, from any tee to any basket. Up to 474 ft.
